# Image placement guide

All website image assets now live under `assets/images/`:

- `brand/` - logos and brand artwork
- `students/` - tutor/student photography currently used on the landing page
- `app/` - app screenshots and preview images
- `placeholders/` - add the five missing images listed below

## Images needed for the remaining placeholders

Use JPG or WebP where possible. Keep each image under 500 KB after compression.

### 1. Kary portrait

Put the image here:

`assets/images/placeholders/kary-portrait.png`

Recommended image:

- A friendly, professional head-and-shoulders photo of Kary
- Square crop
- At least 1000 x 1000 pixels
- Plain or tutoring-related background
- Face centred with comfortable space around the head

This replaces the `Kary's Photo` box on the full About Kary page.

## Photography guidance

- Use real Infinite Solutions sessions when possible.
- Keep lighting bright and natural.
- Avoid visible personal information, school records, or full student names.
- Obtain parent/guardian permission before publishing identifiable minors.
- Keep the four process photos visually consistent in lighting and colour.
- Do not add text to the photos; the website already supplies the labels.

Once these exact filenames are present, update `App.jsx` to import them and replace the current placeholder blocks.
